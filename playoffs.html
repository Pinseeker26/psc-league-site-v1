2026 BB League Playoffs

How the Playoffs Work

📢 Commissioner’s Playoff Notes

Welcome to the 2026 Bogus Boys Playoffs! Before Round 1 begins, please take a minute to understand how the playoff system works.

🏌️ Round 1

Every league member qualifies for Round 1. The format is Individual Low Net, just like we've played all season, except everyone competes together—there are no flights.

🏆 Earning Your Way to the Sweet 16

Your Playoff Re-Seeding Points in the left chart are based on where you finished in the regular season. Next week, you'll earn Round 1 Placement Points from the middle chart based on your finish. Those two point totals are added together to determine the Sweet 16.

⭐ Advancing to the Final Four

The Sweet 16 players will again compete in Individual Low Net. They will earn Sweet 16 Placement Points from the right chart, which determine the Final Four.

👑 Championship Round

The Final Four will compete in one final round of straight Individual Low Net to determine the 2026 Bogus Boys League Champion. No points are used in the Championship Round—the lowest net score wins!

💰 Championship Payouts

Be sure to check out the Final Four payout cards below to see what's up for grabs.

🤔 Why Are the Sweet 16 and Final Four Already Filled In?

The website automatically displays the current projected qualifiers based on the results available at that moment. It is not my prediction! 😄 As each playoff round is completed, the website automatically recalculates and updates the Sweet 16, Final Four, and final standings.

👍 Everyone Keeps Playing

Even if you don't advance to the Sweet 16 or the Final Four, you'll still play every week just like normal. The playoffs determine the league champion, but league play continues for everyone through the end of the season.
