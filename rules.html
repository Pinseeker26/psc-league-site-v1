Rules & Information

Please review these rules before each league round.

1.

PACE OF PLAY

Very simple, keep up with the group in front of you and don't hold up the group behind you! PLAY READY GOLF!

2.

PREFERRED LIE

You can improve your lie, 12" in any direction except closer to the hole. Ball must stay in the area where it lies (ie – Ball is in the rough, ball stays in the rough). LIFT CLEAN & PLACE!

3.

SAND TRAPS

We play sand traps as players choice, if you like hitting out of sand go ahead and hit. If you want to pull it, you must place the ball BEHIND the trap so that the trap is between you and the hole. NO PENALTY!

4.

OUT OF BOUNDS

We don't play distance, if you hit the ball out of bounds, place the ball no more than 2 club lengths from where your ball entered and play from there. 1 STROKE PENALTY!

5.

WATER HAZARD

If your ball goes into the water, place the ball from the point of entry back as far as you want to go unless there is a drop zone. If your ball crosses over the water hazard and rolls back into the water, place the ball no more than 2 club lengths from where your ball entered the water. 1 STROKE PENALTY!

6.

LOST BALL

A ball is considered lost after looking for it for NO LONGER THAN 3 MINUTES. Keep play moving, drop a ball as close as possible to where it was deemed lost and play on. 1 STROKE PENALTY!

7.

GALLERY BALL / PLUGGED BALL / LEAF BALL

It has a bunch of different names and we've all had it happen to us—we hit the ball within the tree line or in the middle of a fairway and when we get there, no ball! After looking for your 3 minutes and discussing with the other players in your group, if they agree that it should not be considered lost, place a ball as close as possible to where you think it should be and play on. NO PENALTY!

8.

HITTING THE WRONG BALL

If you happen to hit the wrong ball, replace the ball where you hit it from and continue playing your own ball. 1 STROKE PENALTY!

9.

GIMMIES

Plain & simple… NO GIMMIES! If it's close enough to be a gimmie, it's close enough to make it.

10.

GOLF ETIQUETTE

Please repair your ball marks, replace or sand your divots, rake bunkers, keep pace with your group, and always show respect to fellow players, course staff, and other golfers. Let's leave each course in better shape than we found it and continue the great reputation the Bogus Boys have built over the years.
