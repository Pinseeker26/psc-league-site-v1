How Your Handicap Is Calculated

Handicaps are formulated by taking your most recent 20 Score Differentials, selecting the lowest 8 differentials, then averaging those 8 and rounding to the nearest tenth. That becomes your Handicap Index.

Score Differential for each round is formulated as follows: Score Differential = (Adjusted Gross Score − Course Rating) × 113 ÷ Slope Rating
